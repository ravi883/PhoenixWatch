Shipping Policy

Last Updated: 6 October 2026

Thank you for shopping with Phoenixwala. This Shipping Policy explains how we process and deliver orders placed through our website.

1. Shipping Locations

Phoenixwala currently ships products to eligible locations within India.

Shipping availability may vary depending on the customer's location and courier service availability.

2. Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after successful order confirmation and payment verification.

Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may be processed on the next business day.

3. Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time is generally 3–7 business days after dispatch.

Delivery times may vary depending on delivery location, courier availability, weather conditions, public holidays, remote-area delivery and unforeseen logistics issues.

The estimated delivery date is not a guaranteed delivery date.

4. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be displayed during checkout before the customer completes the order.

Phoenixwala may offer free shipping for selected products, locations or promotional periods.

5. Order Tracking

Where tracking is available, customers may receive tracking information through email, SMS, WhatsApp or other available communication methods.

6. Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address.

If an incorrect address is provided, delivery may be delayed or the package may be returned.

Additional shipping charges may apply if re-shipping is required because of an incorrect address provided by the customer.

7. Delayed Delivery

Although we make reasonable efforts to deliver orders within the estimated timeframe, delays may occur due to circumstances beyond our control.

  • Weather conditions
  • Natural disasters
  • Courier delays
  • Transportation problems
  • Public holidays
  • Strikes
  • Government restrictions
  • Incorrect address information
  • Other unforeseen circumstances

8. Damaged Package

If your package appears significantly damaged at the time of delivery, please contact Phoenixwala as soon as possible.

Customers may be requested to provide photographs or videos of the package and product for verification.

9. Missing or Lost Shipment

If your order does not arrive within the expected delivery period, please contact us with your order number.

We will coordinate with the courier service to investigate the shipment.
